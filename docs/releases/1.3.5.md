## Status

`candidate`. S1 was read-only confirmed on Debian 12 x86-64 with 2 vCPUs. At `2026-11-20T19:40:52Z`, `/opt/tracker-v1/current` pointed to `1.3.0-73e47a4f`; the live native Tracker reported `1.2.8`, while Web reported `1.3.0`. All four units were active with zero restarts.
