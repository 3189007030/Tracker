## Limits

Capacity and endurance were outside the scope of this release.

In Chromium, shrinking an already rendered desktop homepage directly from 1280 px to 390 px left the history chart grid wider than the viewport until reload. Opening directly at 390 px and switching from 844 px to 390 px kept the page within the viewport.
